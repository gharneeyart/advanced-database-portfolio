# Portfolio 1: Database Design and Modelling

## Case Study: Adetwin Stores POS and Inventory Management System

Adetwin Stores is a packaged foodstuff business that needs a reliable way to manage its products, stock, sales, customers, and payments. The business needs to keep accurate records of the goods it sells and the quantity remaining in stock.

The proposed Point of Sale (POS) and Inventory Management System will store product information, record sales transactions, keep customer records, and track payments made by customers. It will support both fully paid sales and credit sales, including partial payments and unpaid sales.

For credit sales, the system will record the customer involved, the total amount owed, the payments received, and the outstanding balance. The system will also help the business monitor stock levels and maintain consistent records of its daily activities.

The purpose of the database is to organise this information properly, reduce record-keeping errors, and make it easier to retrieve accurate information about sales, payments, customers, and inventory.
